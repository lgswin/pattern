    01처음과 현재의 변화처음에는 A였지만, 시간이 지나면서 B가 되었다At first..., but over time...At first, I wasn't very confident, but over time, I became more comfortable.변화·성장
    02계속하면서 발전계속 A하다 보니 점점 B하게 되었다As I kept..., I gradually...As I kept practicing, I gradually became more confident.변화·성장
    03과거와 현재 비교예전에는 A했지만, 지금은 B한다I used to..., but now...I used to get nervous, but now I can speak more naturally.변화·성장
    04예상과 실제 차이처음에는 A라고 생각했지만, 막상 해보니 B였다I didn't expect..., but...I didn't expect much, but it turned out to be really useful.경험·결과
    05어려움과 배움A하기는 어려웠지만, 그 과정에서 B를 배웠다It was difficult, but I learned...It was harder than I expected, but I learned a lot from the experience.경험·결과
    06계획과 결과계획대로 되지는 않았지만, 결과적으로 B가 되었다It didn't go according to plan, but...It didn't go according to plan, but things worked out well in the end.경험·결과
    07생각의 변화예전에는 A라고 생각했지만, 경험하면서 B라고 깨달았다I used to think..., but I realized...I used to think it wasn't important, but I realized it made a big difference.의견·판단
    08장단점 평가장단점은 있지만, 전반적으로는 B라고 생각한다There are pros and cons, but overall...There are pros and cons, but overall, I think it was a positive experience.의견·판단
    09단기와 장기 비교당장은 A하더라도, 장기적으로는 B가 낫다Even if..., in the long run...Even if it's inconvenient now, I think it's better in the long run.의견·판단
    10현실적인 선택완벽하지는 않지만, 현실적으로는 B가 최선이다It's not perfect, but realistically...It's not a perfect solution, but realistically, it's probably the best option.의견·판단
    11경험 기반 판단내 경험상, A만 봐도 B인지 알 수 있다Based on my experience, I can...Based on my experience, I can usually tell when something is wrong.의견·판단
    12작은 것의 큰 영향사소해 보이지만, 실제로는 큰 영향을 줬다It might seem..., but it made...It might seem like a small thing, but it made a big difference in my daily life.경험·결과
    13익숙해지는 과정A에 익숙해지는 데 시간이 걸렸다It took me a while to get used to...It took me a while to get used to speaking English at work.변화·성장
    14가치 판단처음에는 시간 낭비라고 생각했지만, 나중에는 가치가 있다고 느꼈다At first..., but later I realized it was worth it.At first, I felt like I was wasting my time, but later I realized it was worth it.경험·결과
    15선호와 이유내가 가장 좋아하는 점은 A라는 것이다What I like most is that...What I like most is that it helps me relax.선호·이유
    16원인과 결과A 때문에 B하게 되었다Because of..., I... / It helped me...Because of that experience, I became more confident.경험·결과
    17점진적 변화처음에는 A였지만 점점 B가 되었다I gradually became...I gradually became more comfortable with it.변화·성장
    18뒤늦은 깨달음그때는 몰랐지만, 나중에 A라는 것을 깨달았다I didn't realize it at the time, but later...I didn't realize it at the time, but later I understood how important it was.변화·성장
    19회고와 평가돌이켜보면, A였다고 생각한다Looking back, I think...Looking back, I think it was a really valuable experience.의견·판단
    20조건부 의견A라면 B라고 생각한다If..., I think...If I had the chance, I think I'd do it again.의견·판단

    조립 공식

    변화 과정을 한 문장에 담고 싶다면At first..., but as I kept..., I gradually...

    먼저 자동화할 핵심 6개

    Part 1·2·3에서 두루 재사용하기 좋은 기본 골격입니다.

    CORE 6
    1. At first..., but...
    2. I used to..., but now...
    3. As I kept..., I gradually...
    4. I didn't expect..., but...
    5. Based on my experience...
    6. Looking back, I think...
